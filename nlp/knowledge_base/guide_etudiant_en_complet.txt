GRADING AND EVALUATION SYSTEM
-------------------------------
- Each subject is graded out of 20
- Continuous assessment: 30% (assignments, quizzes, projects)
- Final exam: 70%
- Passing grade: 10/20 per subject
- Semester average: weighted average of all semester subjects

Grade scale:
- 18–20: Excellent (Très Bien)
- 14–17.99: Very Good (Bien)
- 12–13.99: Good (Assez Bien)
- 10–11.99: Satisfactory (Passable)
- < 10: Fail (Échec)

Grade appeals:
- Appeal period: 5 days from official grade publication
- Submit written appeal to the registrar's office (Office 102)
- Review conducted by the professor with department head approval

EXAMS AND RETAKES
------------------
Exam sessions:
- January/February: Semester 1 exams (announced in October)
- May/June: Semester 2 exams (announced in March)
- September: Retake session for failed subjects

Retake rules:
- Students may retake a subject in which they scored below 10/20
- Number of retakable subjects: the entire failed semester
- Grade kept: the higher of the two attempts (regular vs. retake)
- Retakes only concern the final exam, not continuous assessment

Exam room access:
- National ID or passport is mandatory
- Arrive 15 minutes before the exam starts
- No mobile phones or electronic devices allowed

INTERNSHIPS AND FINAL YEAR PROJECT (PFE)
-----------------------------------------
Mandatory internships:
- L2: Observation internship — 4 weeks (summer)
- L3: Technical internship — 6–8 weeks (summer)
- M1: Specialized internship — 2–3 months
- M2: Final year thesis (PFE) — 6 months

Internship procedure:
1. Get the list of partner companies from the Career Office (Office C101)
2. Register your internship with your supervisor
3. Get the internship agreement (Convention) from the faculty (3 copies)
4. Submit the internship report within 15 days of completion
5. Evaluation by the host company + supervising professor

Final year project (PFE):
- Submit thesis topic: November (M2, Year 2)
- Thesis submission: May (June defense session) or August (September session)
- Defense jury: minimum 3 members
- Presentation language: according to program rules (French or English)

Contact: stages@fpst.tn | Office C101

STUDENT REGULATIONS
--------------------
Absences:
- Justified absence: medical or official document required within 48h
- Maximum unjustified absences: 20% of total hours per subject
- Exceeding the limit: exclusion from the final exam in that subject

Core rules:
- Mutual respect between students, professors, and staff
- Care for equipment and campus facilities
- No phone use during class without professor's permission
- Smoking prohibited inside all buildings
- Zero tolerance for any form of cheating

Cheating penalties:
- Proven cheating: zero on the exam + referral to disciplinary committee
- Repeated cheating: temporary or permanent expulsion depending on severity

Grievances:
- Complaint about a professor: send to the Pedagogical Director
- Complaint about administration: send to the Faculty Director
- Response deadline: maximum 10 working days
