PROFESSOR CATEGORIES AND CONTRACTS
------------------------------------
Full-time permanent professors:
- Permanent contract (CDI) after 2-year trial period
- Annual teaching load: 384 ETD hours (equivalent TD)
- Gross salary: 2,800 to 4,500 DT/month depending on rank and seniority
- Benefits: health insurance, research bonus, dedicated office, lab access
- Obligations: participation in academic councils and thesis juries

Part-time permanent professors:
- 192 ETD hours/year
- Pro-rated salary
- Possible to combine with another position (written authorization required)

Adjunct/Visiting lecturers:
- Hourly rate: 35 to 55 DT/hour depending on qualification
  * Master's graduate / PhD student: 35 DT/h
  * PhD / Experienced engineer: 45 DT/h
  * Senior expert / Consultant: 55 DT/h
- Contract signed at start of semester, payment at end of semester or monthly
- Maximum: 192h/year per adjunct lecturer at FPST

WORKLOAD DISTRIBUTION (ETD)
-----------------------------
The 384h ETD load includes:
- 1h lecture (CM) = 1.5h ETD
- 1h tutorial (TD) = 1h ETD
- 1h lab (TP) = 0.67h ETD
- Thesis supervision: 10h ETD per student supervised (max 5 students)
- Jury participation: 2h ETD per jury

Overtime hours: triggered above 384h ETD for permanent professors
Overtime rate: 45 DT/hour (tax-exempt up to 96h/year)
Director's prior approval required.

SALARY SCALE (CDI)
-------------------
Assistant lecturer (< 3 years): 2,800 – 3,200 DT/month
Confirmed assistant (3–7 years): 3,200 – 3,800 DT/month
Associate professor (> 7 years, PhD): 3,800 – 4,500 DT/month
Full professor (> 10 years, publications): 4,500 – 5,500 DT/month

Regular bonuses:
- Semester performance bonus: 150–400 DT (based on student evaluations)
- Research bonus: 200 DT per article published in indexed journal
- Annual bonus: 1 month salary (for CDI > 1 year)
- Transport allowance: 80 DT/month
- Health insurance: 70% covered by FPST

LEAVE AND ABSENCE POLICY
--------------------------
Annual leave (CDI): 30 working days/year
Summer leave: July–August (subject to exam retake sessions)
Christmas/Eid holidays: aligned with official academic calendar

Academic absences (outside teaching load):
- Conferences/symposia: up to 5 days/year with Director's approval
- Continuing education: up to 10 days/year
- International expert missions: HR + Direction file required

Unplanned absence procedure:
1. Notify pedagogical secretary (Office 202) as soon as possible
2. Propose a make-up class within 72 hours
3. Notify students via Moodle or official email
Note: Unrecovered absence = salary deduction (1/22 per day)

ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURES
---------------------------
Leave request:
1. Fill in the leave request form (available from the secretary)
2. Submit to department head at least 15 days before the requested date
3. Department head approves and forwards to management
4. Official response within 5 working days

Types of leave:
- Annual leave: 30 working days/year
- Sick leave: medical certificate required within 48h
- Maternity/paternity leave: per Tunisian Labor Code
- Unpaid leave: exceptional request to submit to management

Absence and make-up classes:
- In case of unplanned absence: notify secretary at least 2 hours before the class
- Propose a make-up session the following week
- Coordinate with department head for room booking
- Students must be informed 24h in advance

Missions and travel:
1. Submit a mission file to management (mission form + program)
2. Minimum 10 days before departure
3. Expense reimbursement: with receipts (tickets, accommodation)
4. Mission report to be submitted within 5 days of return

EXAM ORGANIZATION
------------------
The professor is responsible for:
- Preparing the exam subject (submitted to secretary 48h in advance)
- Invigilation (schedule set by department head)
- Grading within set deadlines (48h for exam sessions)
- Submitting grades to the registrar

Grade publication:
- Grades are published via the FPST SmartDesk system after department head validation
- Students consult their grades in their personal space
- No grade may be communicated verbally before official publication

Student grade appeals:
- Students may contest a grade within 5 days of publication
- Student submits review request to department head
- Professor must provide a copy of the correction within 48h

TEACHING SCHEDULE AND ROOMS
-----------------------------
- Timetable managed by the Planning Department (Bureau 201)
- Schedules available on FPST SmartDesk platform
- Room requests: at least 48h in advance via Room Booking Form
- Computer lab: reservation required (Office 301)
- Changes to timetable: notify Planning Department and students at least 24h in advance

GRADE ENTRY
------------
Grade entry in the FPST SmartDesk system:
- Access via: elearning.fpst.tn with your institutional credentials
- Entry deadline: 48h after the last exam
- Grading: /20, with 10/20 as passing grade
- Continuous assessment counts for 30% of final grade

Steps:
1. Log into the FPST SmartDesk platform
2. Go to "My Courses" → select the module
3. Click "Grades" → enter each student's grade
4. Submit and click "Validate"
Note: Once validated, grades can only be modified with department head approval

THESIS SUPERVISION (PFE)
--------------------------
Supervision load:
- Maximum 5 PFE students per professor per year
- Each supervision = 10h ETD (counted in annual workload)
- Additional supervision: 45 DT/h compensation

Defense calendar:
- June defenses: thesis submitted by May 31st
- September defenses: thesis submitted by August 31st
- Jury composition: minimum 3 members (supervisor + 2 others)

Online form submission: elearning.fpst.tn → "PFE Management"

HEALTH AND SAFETY IN LABS
---------------------------
Security rules:
- Mandatory PPE (gloves, goggles) in chemistry and electronics labs
- 1 fire extinguisher per lab, emergency exit clearly marked
- First aid kit: accessible at all times
- MSDS (Material Safety Data Sheet): available for every chemical product

Incident/accident reporting:
1. Immediately secure the area
2. Call emergency: 15 or Internal: Ext. 9
3. Fill in the accident report within 24h (Office 103)
4. Preventive maintenance: first Tuesday of every month (lab closed)

INTERNAL REGULATIONS FOR PROFESSORS
--------------------------------------
Obligations:
- Be present at least 15 minutes before the first class of the day
- Keep attendance sheets up to date
- Submit exam subjects 48h before the exam
- Submit grades within 48h after the last exam
- Attend compulsory institutional meetings (jury, academic council)
- Respect confidentiality of student information

Prohibited:
- Using institutional equipment for personal purposes without authorization
- Disclosing individual student grades to third parties
- Accepting any gift or benefit from students
- Communicating grades before official publication

Academic integrity:
- Student plagiarism must be reported to the department head
- Plagiarism detection tool available on Moodle (Turnitin)
- Plagiarism penalty: zero grade on the work + possible disciplinary action

ANNUAL EVALUATION OF TEACHING STAFF
--------------------------------------
Annual evaluation by:
1. Pedagogical Director (individual interview, June)
2. Students (anonymous questionnaire on Moodle, end of semester)
3. Self-evaluation (annual activity report, due before June 30th)

Evaluation criteria:
- Teaching quality (clarity, availability, materials)
- Student pass rate
- Compliance with deadlines (grade publication, exam submission)
- Participation in institutional life (councils, juries, supervision)
- Research activities and publications

Consequences:
- Score ≥ 4/5: automatic annual increase (2–3%)
- Score 3–4/5: improvement plan proposed
- Score < 3/5 for two consecutive years: HR disciplinary meeting
